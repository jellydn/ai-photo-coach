Built-in coaching, not filters

Three things that actually help you take better photos.

Live composition guides

Rule of thirds, horizon level, center marker, and headroom framing — drawn on top of your preview so you can line up the shot before you shoot.

Smart coaching prompts

Short, plain-language tips — "Step back", "Face the light", "Hold steady" — appear once at a time and fade as you fix the issue.

Auto Best Shot

A live 0–100 readiness score watches framing, lighting, and stability. When it hits 80 and the phone is steady, a 3-2-1 countdown fires the shot for you.

Three steps

How AI Photo Coach works.

1

Point and see

Open the app and pick a mode. Guides, the readiness score, and coaching prompts appear directly on your camera preview.

2

Follow the score

0–100 updates live as you adjust framing, level the horizon, and wait for steady hands. Each prompt clears the instant you fix it.

3

Capture automatically

When the score is 80+ and the shot is steady, the 3-2-1 countdown triggers the shutter — no button needed.
